## Lessons from the bench
- **Thickness resonance: fs = c / (2t).** A PZT disc rings at the frequency where its thickness is half a
  wavelength. Worked (this lab's disc): PZT-5A, c~4400 m/s, t~1.0 mm -> fs~2.2 MHz; the measured capacitance
  back-solved t~0.94 mm -> ~2.0 MHz. So the electrical impedance sweep (Module 06) and this physics agree.
- Thinner disc = higher frequency; the ~2 MHz choice is a skull-penetration vs resolution tradeoff (Module 10).
