## Purpose
Dissolved-O2 readout to verify the water is degassed before acoustic measurements.

## Basic operating principle
Membrane (Clark-type) dissolved-oxygen probe. Reads DO in mg/L and/or % saturation. Used to confirm the
tank water is degassed before acoustic measurements.

## Typical settings
- **Air/saturation-calibrate at the start of each session**; let the membrane probe **polarize** first.
- **Stir or move the probe** while reading - stagnant water reads falsely low. Let **temp equilibrate**.
- Degas **target: DO < ~2-3 mg/L (<~20-30% saturation)**. Practical resolution floor **+/-0.4 mg/L** - don't
  over-interpret sub-0.4 differences.

## Safety considerations
Keep the membrane wet between uses; replace electrolyte/membrane per the manual if readings drift or won't
calibrate.
