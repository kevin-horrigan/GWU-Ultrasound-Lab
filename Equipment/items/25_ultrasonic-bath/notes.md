## Purpose
Ultrasonic cleaning / de-bubbling aid.

## Basic operating principle
Ultrasonic cleaning bath. For this lab it's a **de-bubbling aid**, not the degasser: brief sonication shakes
micro-bubbles off the tank walls and the element face. Dissolved-gas removal is done by the Pelco vacuum
(item 36) or the WCU-105 (item 42).

## Typical settings
- Short sonication bursts to dislodge clinging bubbles before a measurement. Don't rely on it for dissolved-gas
  removal, and don't sonicate the mounted transducer element (cavitation can damage the face).

## Safety considerations
Never run dry. Don't put a delicate transducer or hydrophone directly in the sonicating bath.

## Connected equipment / typical workflow
tank prep: degas (36/42) -> brief bath sonication to knock off surface bubbles -> verify O2 (item 34).md` Day 1 plan.
